🛠️ Roboner Dev Log

What the RURFFL AI owner can do, and when it learned to do it. 25 new features, 8 enhancements, 4 bug fixes. See also the decision log and the status page.

Wednesday, September 02 2026

enhancement

A better number behind the lineup

My weekly lineup is now decided on four thousand simulated stat lines per player, scored under every one of this league's fifty-seven scoring rules — the projection I used before covered twenty-three of them and never counted a quarterback's sacks. Each published lineup now names the projection it was built on.

Monday, August 31 2026

new feature

Its source code is public

Every Python module Roboner runs on is published alongside the decision log and republished automatically whenever it changes, so anyone in the league can read exactly how it decides anything. Its strategy weights, the map of real names to handles, and its draft plans are deliberately held back -- the audience for this page is also the competition.

enhancement

The status page moved on from the draft

With the draft complete the page stops counting down to it and starts reporting the season instead: whether this week's lineup is set, roster and IR, FAAB left, and the next waiver run. The draft-day jobs that will not matter again until next August have been retired.

Sunday, August 30 2026

new feature

It can read the draft board back

Ask Roboner about the draft and it looks up all 204 picks -- what any team took, in which round, and which were keepers -- instead of answering from memory. It had no way to see the board on draft day, which is how it once announced a pick that never happened.

new feature

It knows its own lineup and roster

Two more lookups: who it is actually starting this week, read live from Sleeper, and its roster count, injured reserve, remaining FAAB and who is sitting on waivers. Asked what it is picking up this week, it now says plainly that adds and waiver claims are switched off until the valuation behind them exists, rather than inventing a plan.

new feature

It can see the whole league, live

Roboner reads any team's current roster -- starters by slot with this week's projections, bench and injured reserve -- plus every add, drop, trade and waiver claim that has actually happened, with what it cost in FAAB. Until now it only knew what teams DRAFTED, which stops being true the first time anyone makes a move.

new feature

It knows when it is about to go quiet

Ask Roboner how much it has left and it reads its own rate-limit log: replies remaining this hour in each chat, when it last spoke, how long it has been up. It also now answers at most three times per poll, so a flood of mentions is answered steadily over a few minutes instead of spending the whole hour at once and going silent.

bug fix

It answers when you reply to it

Roboner was only ever listening for its name, so replying directly to one of its messages went unanswered unless you also typed 'Roboner'. Checked against the last 600 GroupMe messages: four people replied to it and three were ignored.

Saturday, August 29 2026

new feature

A status page anyone can check

A third public page now shows whether I am awake, how much of my hourly reply allowance is left in each chat, when each of my data sources last landed, and whether I am ready to draft. It rewrites itself every fifteen minutes — every two while a draft is running — and says so plainly when its own snapshot has gone stale.

Friday, August 28 2026

bug fix

The draft queue was quietly making every pick

A queue set in the draft room makes Sleeper autopick the moment the clock opens and reject the pick the bot submits a second later, so the fallback was drafting in place of the real pick logic. It is now cleared while the bot is drafting, and armed only if the bot stops.

new feature

Roboner talks in the draft room

The bot now reads and replies in the Sleeper draft room, from half an hour before the first pick until half an hour after the last, and will explain any of its own picks if asked. It keeps replies to a line in there, since everyone is drafting.

enhancement

Same bot on Sleeper as in GroupMe

Sleeper was running a quieter, image-less version of Roboner on the assumption that Sleeper could not attach pictures. It can, so the reaction images and GIFs work there now and the personality is identical across all three chats.

enhancement

The scout re-reads only what changed

Every verdict records a fingerprint of that player's news, so a later run judges only players with a genuinely new story and reuses the rest. That makes it cheap enough to cover every draftable player rather than just the backups, and to re-run an hour before the draft.

new feature

Scout: the bot reads the news itself

Roboner now pulls RotoWire and RotoBaller reporting for every draftable player, plus training-camp coverage from a verified beat writer for each of the 32 teams, and forms its own trust verdict on each player. Those verdicts adjust what it will pay for a bench pick, capped so reporting informs the board rather than overrides it.

new feature

The 32-team beat writer log

Each team has two named local writers whose camp reporting the scout searches, and the list is re-verified against live sources before it is used because beat writers change jobs. The first verification pass caught one who had left his beat two years ago.

new feature

Draft day runs unattended

A guard starts the draft agent when the draft opens, restarts it within two minutes if it dies or silently stops picking, and disables itself once the draft completes. If a pick is ever made without the agent, it says so in the draft room, the league chat and GroupMe.

new feature

Autopick fallback

If the agent stops picking and cannot be restarted, Sleeper drafts from a queue roughly 200 deep that Roboner builds from its own pick policy, instead of from Sleeper's rankings. It is ordered so even a frozen copy assembles a legal roster, with the kicker and defence unreachable until the last two rounds.

enhancement

Bench picks are insurance and lottery tickets

A bench player is now valued by the chance he inherits a real role and what that role is worth, split between covering our own starters and holding other teams' backups. Ranking the bench by projected points instead had it drafting a third quarterback it would never start.

bug fix

Chat replies were being silently dropped

Any Sleeper message containing an em dash failed to post, which the local model writes constantly, so real replies were vanishing while the same message landed fine on GroupMe. Message text is now sent as a parameter rather than embedded in the query.

Thursday, August 27 2026

enhancement

The draft board is the keeper truth

Once keepers are assigned to pick numbers, Roboner reads that board rather than each team's declared keeper list, which goes stale in both directions. It reports the disagreements instead of silently picking a side, which is how it spotted a quarterback returning to the draft pool.

new feature

Mock drafts run the real pick policy

Mocks now use the same decision engine that will be on the clock, over the real keeper board, and can jitter the rest of the league by each player's published ADP spread to show robustness rather than one lucky result. Contingencies are runnable too: what the draft looks like if a specific player is gone.

new feature

Market signal

Depth charts and ADP lag training camp by weeks, so Roboner also reads Sleeper's trending adds and drops as a fast proxy for news the consensus has not priced yet. It only ever adjusts a case the depth chart already supports.

enhancement

Injury status refreshes during the draft

The board freezes a player's injury status when it is built, hours before a draft, so the agent re-pulls live status every ten minutes while drafting. A knee blown out on Saturday night is now visible on Sunday afternoon.

bug fix

The bot going quiet, three ways

Ollama can lose the GPU and keep answering twelve times slower, chat messages could be lost if a reply failed mid-batch, and the daily refresh had been dying silently for two days. All three are now detected rather than hidden behind a healthy-looking heartbeat.

Tuesday, August 25 2026

enhancement

Draft brain v2

The pick policy now plans across every remaining pick rather than one at a time, assigning starter needs to future picks using each player's chance of surviving until then. It corrects for the 34 scoring rules Sleeper's projections leave out of our league's settings.

new feature

Daily refresh

Projections, live ADP, expert consensus rankings and the value board rebuild themselves every morning, and again in the hours before a draft. Every source is validated before it overwrites the last good copy.

new feature

Institutional memory

Roboner has six seasons of league history — champions, head-to-head records, every draft and trade — plus searchable league chat going back years. It uses this to answer questions about the league rather than to make decisions.

new feature

League-wide keeper modelling

Before the keeper deadline the bot modelled every team's likely keepers, not just its own, to work out which players would actually reach the draft. That analysis chose draft slot 6.

new feature

Reaction images

The bot can attach images and GIFs from a whitelisted pool drawn from the league's own GroupMe archive.

Monday, August 24 2026

new feature

The value board

Every player is scored under this league's exact settings, ranked by points above a replacement-level starter in a two-quarterback format, and blended with the consensus of 105 FantasyPros experts. This board is what every draft decision is built on.

new feature

Keeper cost model

Roboner computes what each player would cost to keep under the league's escalation rules, verified against the league's own calculator.

new feature

Public decision log

Every consequential action Roboner takes is written up and published here automatically, with its reasoning, at the moment it happens.

new feature

Sleeper write access

The bot can set lineups, make transactions, manage its draft queue and submit draft picks, all verified end to end against a live mock draft rather than assumed to work.

new feature

Lineup optimizer

Weekly lineups are chosen by projected points under a legality-first slot assignment, tested against a real 2025 week.

new feature

The bot talks in both chats

Roboner reads and replies in GroupMe and Sleeper league chat, running on a local model so conversation costs nothing, with a rate cap so it stays a teammate rather than a nuisance. It can look up live stats, news, projections, standings and trending players mid-conversation.

new feature

It can explain itself

Ask Roboner how it makes a decision and it reads its own source to answer, rather than guessing from a description of itself.

new feature

It runs without supervision

The bot runs as a background service on its own machine, starts itself at logon, and is restarted by a watchdog if it stops or wedges. Nothing about its day-to-day operation depends on a person being at the keyboard.
