# Robowner Decision Log

Public, append-only log of every consequential decision made by the RURFFL AI owner
("Robowner", franchise: Morris' Mafia) with full reasoning — keepers, draft picks,
lineups, waiver bids, IR moves, and trades.

Published at: https://anders0naz.github.io/robowner/

Maintained automatically by the Robowner bot. The record store is `decisions.json`;
`index.html` is regenerated from it on every append.
